# Plio Callizer – Automatic Call Analyzer

## Overview

Plio Callizer is an automated call analysis tool designed to help marketing and business teams evaluate customer calls and extract useful insights from conversations.

## Key Features

* Automatically analyzes recorded customer calls.
* Converts call conversations into structured text for analysis.
* Identifies important customer requirements, queries, and discussion points.
* Helps marketing teams understand customer interactions and recurring topics.
* Organizes call insights for easier review and reporting.
* Reduces manual effort involved in reviewing large numbers of customer calls.

## Marketing Use Case

The tool can support marketing and business teams by analyzing customer conversations to identify:

* Customer needs and interests
* Frequently asked questions
* Common objections
* Lead-related information
* Customer feedback
* Recurring conversation trends

## Technologies

* Python
* AI/LLM-based text analysis
* Data Processing
* Speech/Conversation Analysis
* Excel/CSV-based reporting

## Outcome

Plio Callizer helps transform unstructured customer-call data into actionable insights, enabling teams to understand customer conversations more efficiently and support data-driven marketing decisions.
